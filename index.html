What is this?

When the AMC moved online, the real test ran on the Art of Problem Solving website. This simulator copies that environment as closely as possible: a countdown timer, one question at a time, A–E bubbles on the left, and automatic grading with a full review afterwards. Problems are real past MAA AMC problems, sourced from LIVE by Po-Shen Loh's public past-contest pages and cross-checked against the AoPS Wiki answer keys. It is a practice tool, not affiliated with MAA or AoPS.

How do I use it?

Click Start exam and your 75-minute timer starts. Click a bubble (A–E) to select it; click the same bubble again to unselect it and leave the question blank. Use Prev / Next or the arrow keys to move, A–E keys to answer, F to flag a question to come back to. The question palette shows answered (blue), current (outlined), and flagged (⚑) questions. When you finish, click Submit test — or just let the timer run out. Your score and a full breakdown appear on the review screen.

Minute plan (shown live during the test): 0–3 scan all 25 · 3–28 Pass 1 · 28–58 Pass 2 · 58–68 Pass 3 · 68–75 check only. The ✓ counter records how many you have solved at the 28- and 58-minute checkpoints — the fix for the AMC 10 A miscount (14 solved, counted as 15).

Scoring & review

Correct: 6 points. Blank: 1.5 points. Wrong: 0. A target of 15–16 clean solves is roughly 100–108 points. On the review screen, every missed problem gets a cause label — knowledge (didn't know the concept), recognition (knew it but didn't spot it), execution (set it up, slipped somewhere), or time (ran out or skipped under pressure) — so the next mock can target the real bottleneck instead of re-studying everything. Results are saved in this browser only (localStorage); nothing is uploaded anywhere.
